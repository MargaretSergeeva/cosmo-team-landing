Five legal gaps

Each can lead to fines or lost contracts.

BDSG §38

No Data Protection Officer

Required at 20+ staff handling personal data.

Evidence: none named in privacy policy

DSGVO Art. 30

No record of processing

Authorities can request it at any time.

Evidence: requested, none exists

DSGVO Art. 28

4 processing agreements missing

AI Act Art. 4

No AI rules or training

Evidence: 3 trackers load before consent
